Wild Botanist privacy policy

For the Wild Botanist app (package com.minormending.wildbotanist), by Standard Mending Co., version 0.1.0 and later. Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Wild Botanist is a single-player idle game about finding, catching and growing plants, for players 13 and over. You don't need an account to play, the app has no advertising, and this version has no sign-in. Your garden is kept on your device.

Play counts (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn how the game is played (for example, how many players finish the opening or come back the next day), the app can send anonymous play counts to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service Google runs for us. This happens only if you told the app you are 13 or over and the "Play counts" switch in Options is on. If you are under 13, or you chose not to give your age, nothing is ever sent.

When it is on, the app sends: a random app-instance identifier that Firebase creates on your device; events such as starting the game, reaching a step of the story, a first win or catch, finishing an episode, opening the app, and whether you allowed notifications; and the standard details Firebase adds, such as the device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate location (country or region) that Google works out from your IP address. It never sends your name, the name you give your botanist, your email, contacts or advertising ID, and it is never used for advertising.

Turning the switch off stops the counts and deletes the app-instance identifier on your device. Google keeps the counts for the retention period set in our analytics settings and no longer than 14 months. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

What stays on your device

Your garden, plants, progress, purchases history and settings are kept in the app's storage on your device and are not sent to us. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may back up the app's data to your Google account so a new phone can restore it; you control that in Android settings.

The app asks your year of birth once, to choose the right settings for your age. It keeps only your age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or adult), on the device. The year itself is not saved and never leaves the device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on your device (for example, when a plot is ready or a new species was caught). It doesn't use a push notification service or send any identifier for notifications. You can turn them off in Options or in Android settings.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app receives only the confirmation of what you bought (the product and when), which it uses to unlock it and keeps on your device. We never see your payment details.

What we do not collect

Wild Botanist does not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, files, hardware device identifiers or advertising ID. It has no ads and no other third-party analytics or tracking.

Children

Wild Botanist is for players 13 and over. If a player says they are under 13 or doesn't give their age, the app sends no play counts at all, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and deletion

Turn play counts off at any time in Options. To delete your garden, clear Wild Botanist's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. There is no account to delete in this version; see Delete your Wild Botanist data. To ask us to delete play counts linked to your app-instance identifier, email us (below).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change. If a later version adds sign-in or cloud saves, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
