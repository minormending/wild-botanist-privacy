Delete your Wild Botanist data

For the Wild Botanist app (package com.minormending.wildbotanist), by Standard Mending Co. This version has no accounts: your garden is kept only on your device.

Your garden

Clear Wild Botanist's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. That deletes your garden, progress and settings from the phone.

Play counts

If you are 13 or over and left "Play counts" on in Options, the app sent anonymous play counts to Google Analytics for Firebase (see the privacy policy). Turn the switch off in Options to stop them and delete the identifier on your device. To ask us to delete counts already sent, email kkramdath@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Wild Botanist data"; we do it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

If accounts are added later

If a later version lets you sign in to keep your garden on several devices, this page will explain how to delete that account in the app and without it.
